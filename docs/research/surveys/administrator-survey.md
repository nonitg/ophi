watching the first campus respondents think aloud, or move them into the follow-up interview. Question
IDs are intentionally stable so exports remain comparable if an optional probe is later hidden.

coordinator, billing or insurance coordinator, or office/practice manager. Route by tasks, not title.

## Introduction

### How work moves through the dental office

We are studying how dental clinics move from a treatment decision through cost or coverage questions to
scheduled care. We want to learn what actually happens, including processes that work well.

This questionnaire takes about 7–9 minutes. It is research, not a test of your clinic. Your responses
will be kept confidential and reported without your name. Please do not enter patient names, initials,
dates of birth, chart numbers, exact appointment dates, or other identifying details.

We will describe a possible software workflow only near the end.

### Concept card

Now consider a possible workflow. A documentation assistant would:

- read the treatment plan and available chart evidence;
- flag items as present, missing, stale, or unable to verify against a cited rule;
- show the evidence behind each flag;
- ask the dentist to record named, time-stamped clinical judgments it cannot infer;
- assemble a review packet; and
- leave review, sign-off, and submission to the clinic. It would not transmit to a payer.
