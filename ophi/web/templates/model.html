What it can't do

  • Change a CDCP requirement or Ophi's result. The rule pack decides, and every fix cites a clause.
  • See X-rays. Criteria judged on a film are pre-filled from the note only, and name the film to check.
  • Record an answer. A pre-fill counts only once the dentist confirms it under their licence.
  • Write to your PMS, or send anything to Sun Life.
