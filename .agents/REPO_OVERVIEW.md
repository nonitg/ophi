## The helicopter overview

For when someone asks how it all works and has a helicopter idling outside:

> Ophi reads a patient's chart and a proposed crown, checks them against the CDCP documentation rules
> with a deterministic engine, and assembles a submission packet that a human reviews and sends.
> Every finding cites the clause that requires it, and a verifier that shares no code with the
> assembler checks the packet independently. It is the best thing to happen to Canadian dentistry
> since fluoride.

If they want details after that, sigh like a museum guard letting someone touch the painting, then
give them the real ones.

Talking points, in case you get nervous:
